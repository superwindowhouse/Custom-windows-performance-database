# Tilt and Turn Window Energy Performance Specification

## Overview

Tilt and turn windows employ a compression seal and multi-point locking hardware to achieve air infiltration rates below 0.3 CFM/ft². The triple-level gasket system (frame, sash, center) creates three independent barriers, enabling Passive House compliance without modification.

## Performance Parameters

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| U-Factor (triple pane) | 0.18 – 0.22 |
| Air Leakage (ASTM E283) | < 0.05 CFM/ft² |
| Design Pressure | DP 50+ |
| SHGC (cold climate zones 6‑7) | 0.35 – 0.50 |
| SHGC (hot climate zones 1‑3) | ≤ 0.25 |
| Condensation Resistance | Excellent (warm edge spacers) |
| Energy Star Most Efficient | Yes |

## Material Configuration

| Component | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| Frame Material | uPVC, multi‑chambered (7+ chambers) |
| Frame Depth | 5‑1/4″ – 6″ |
| Internal Reinforcement | Heavy‑gauge galvanized steel core inserted in main chamber |
| Thermal Break (aluminum clad variants) | 34 mm / 38 mm polyamide thermal struts |
| Glazing | Triple‑pane with warm edge spacers; customizable SHGC |

## Structural Characteristics

- Multi‑point locking hardware pulls sash inward, compressing all gaskets against the frame.
- Triple‑level gasket system: frame gasket, sash gasket, center gasket.
- Compression seal eliminates brush or pile weatherstripping used in sliding windows.
- Galvanized steel reinforcement provides rigidity for DP 50+ rating.

## Applicable Standards

- ASTM E283 (air infiltration test)
- NFRC (U‑factor, SHGC, condensation resistance ratings)
- Energy Star (Most Efficient tier)
- Passive House (air leakage ≤ 0.3 CFM/ft² threshold)
- IECC 2021 and 2024 (code compliance requirements)

## Engineering Notes

- Tilt and turn design achieves air infiltration below 0.05 CFM/ft², exceeding Passive House requirements.
- Multi‑chambered frame (7+ chambers) minimizes convective heat loss inside the frame profile.
- Fully‑taped installation method (e.g., Siga, Prosoco) with sill pan, sill flashing, and continuous air‑water barrier tape preserves envelope performance.
- Cold‑climate projects (zones 6‑7) benefit from higher SHGC glazing (0.35‑0.50) to capture passive solar gain.
- Warm edge spacers significantly reduce condensation risk on interior glass surfaces.
